# Swift Co-op Mini Apps

These are apps I built while working through Apple's **App Development with Swift** curriculum.

| App | Course unit | What I built | Open |
|---|---|---|---|
| **QuestionBot** | 2 – Algorithms | A UIKit app that answers typed questions. My `MyQuestionAnswerer` logic gives custom replies to "when / where / who / what / why", to greetings, and to time-of-day messages, and falls back to defaults based on question length. | `QuestionBot/QuestionBot.xcodeproj` |
| **ColorMix** | 4 – Building Apps | A UIKit color mixer. Red, green, and blue switches turn each color on or off, sliders set how much of each, and a preview square shows the mixed color. Sliders are disabled while their color is off, and Reset returns everything to the start. | `ColorMix/ColorMix.xcodeproj` |
| **BouncyBall** | 3 – Organizing Data | A physics game: tap the funnel to drop a ball, bounce it off barriers, and hit every target. My game logic is in `GameCode.swift`. The `Common/` shape and scene engine is provided by the course. | `BouncyBall/BouncyBall.xcodeproj` |
